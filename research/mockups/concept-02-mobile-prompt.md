```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a high-fidelity original Korean mobile wedding invitation design presentation: three adjacent flat tall mobile web screens of ONE coherent concept called "TOGETHER, EVERYWHERE". This is a creative alternative to an OTT/social-feed invitation, made entirely from still photos and text, NO videos.
Concept: an intimate blue travel journal of two people, photographs tucked into translucent album pockets, cobalt handwritten annotations, small mustard date stamp. The signature is a gracefully curved cobalt route-line connecting the two people's small candid portraits and arriving at a cut-paper wedding invitation ticket. No actual maps or airplane boarding-pass gimmicks. Photographs of an invented adult Korean couple, consistent faces; documentary natural light, one wedding portrait, one casual travel photo, one detail of hands.
Canvas landscape approximately 1800x1500. Three parallel phone-size UI viewports roughly 390x844, flat edge-to-edge screenshots with NO phone hardware or perspective, gaps 30px, pale neutral surrounding board. Outside panel labels "첫 화면 · 390", "사진 이야기 · 390", "예식 안내 · 390". Keep important text realistically readable for a 390px mobile viewport: body about 16px equivalent, buttons at least 48px tall. Mobile safe-area bottom padding. Small top labels inside screens, no navbar crammed with links.
Palette: mist blue #E5EFF5, cobalt ink #2448B5, deep navy #182846, sunflower yellow #F4D263, clean white #FFFFFF. No cream-black editorial default, purple gradients, flowers, hearts everywhere, neon or glass dashboard.
Typography: expressive Korean calligraphic but readable display headlines, dark clean Korean sans for body, tiny restrained monospaced dates. Headlines carry personality with hand-underlined words. Keep decorative handwriting to short marginal notes.
LEFT / cover: mist-blue paper background with slight fine grain. Small header "지훈과 서연의 기록". Big memorable Korean headline "같이 걷다," on first line and "함께 살기로." on second line, cobalt brush underscore. Beneath, a large warmly lit still wedding photo in a white instant-photo border tilted only 2 degrees, not covering any text. A second small cropped casual photograph partly peeks behind the first, in a translucent blue album pocket. Tiny handwritten marginal note "우리의 다음 여행". A thin cobalt curved route line moves down toward a perforated-edge invitation ticket near the lower third with exact text "2027.05.22 SAT", "오후 2시 · 가든홀", "지훈 & 서연". Bottom two calm actions "사진 이야기" and "예식 안내", all fully visible. No opening gate. Names date and place visible immediately.
MIDDLE / photo story: white with pale-blue ruled album margins. Header "우리가 모은 장면들" and subtitle "특별하지 않아서 더 좋았던 날들". A vertical photo-journal composition, generous air: large 4:3 travel photo of the couple walking near sea, blue handwritten caption "함께라면 어디든". Below, one small portrait and one landscape detail arranged in an intentional asymmetric collage contained fully inside the screen. A curved blue line connects 3 small circular timeline dots next to captions "처음 만난 날", "같이 떠난 날", "같은 마음이 된 날". A small horizontal strip of photo thumbnails at the bottom with the next photo partially visible to suggest swiping, and explicit text action "사진 크게 보기". No play buttons, no video thumbnails, no like or follower counts.
RIGHT / event info: pale-blue background. Header "우리의 다음 목적지". Big white vertically stacked paper invitation card with clipped corners and one subtle yellow date stamp. Center title "결혼식에 초대합니다", couple names "지훈 & 서연", clear date "2027년 5월 22일 토요일", time "오후 2시". A simple small May calendar with 22 circled in cobalt; if calendar is rendered, correct May 2027 grid starts Saturday with 1, and 22 is Saturday. Clearly separated venue name "가든홀" and "서울 · 예시 장소". Two large full-width buttons "지도에서 보기" in cobalt white text and "주소 복사" outline below. Quiet collapsible row "마음 전하실 곳" with plus icon. Bottom compact bar with text buttons "예식 안내" and "연락하기" with clear safe-area padding.
Constraints: a realistic mobile website, no impossible overlapping functional text, no sideways entire website, no wide desktop cards shrunk onto phones, no video or audio controls, no fake QR. Only still photography. All event data and people fictional. Polish and originality of a small independent design studio, tactile gentle youthful elegant. Outer footer "CONCEPT 02 · PHOTO JOURNAL · MOBILE FIRST".
```
